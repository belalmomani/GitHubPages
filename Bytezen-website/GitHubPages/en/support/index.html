Support & FAQ

Does Bytezen work without the PC program?

No. The app is a remote control; you need the free Diskati companion on a Windows PC. Without it the app only shows the pairing screens.

Are my files uploaded to the internet?

No. Your phone talks directly to your PC on your local network over TLS with a pinned PC-certificate fingerprint. We have no servers and no accounts.

Which systems are supported?

Android 7.0+ phones and Windows 10 or 11 PCs.

Is the app free?

Yes, free and supported by ads via Start.io. See the privacy policy for what the ad library collects.

Is deletion permanent?

No. “Move to quarantine” isolates the file and you can restore it with an integrity check. Permanent deletion is a separate, explicit action.

Why does Windows show a warning when I run diskati.exe?

The file is not signed with a paid code-signing certificate, so SmartScreen warns about any new unknown program. Download only from the official releases page and compare the SHA-256 with SHA256SUMS.txt published with the release.

My phone cannot find my PC. What should I do?

Make sure both are on the same network, the program is running (tray icon near the clock), and you approved the firewall/administrator prompt on first run. If the PC's address changes, the app searches your local network automatically.

How many phones can I pair?

Up to 10. You can unpair any phone from the program's tray menu.

How do I delete my data?

We hold none on servers. The data-deletion page explains how to remove everything stored on your phone and PC.
