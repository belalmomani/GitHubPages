Bytezen features

Everything you need to manage your Windows PC's storage from your phone.

Disk space map

Rectangles sized by folder; tap to drill in and spot the biggest consumers at a glance.

Temp cleanup

Removes temp files older than 24 hours and skips files in use.

Safe quarantine

Move files to quarantine instead of deleting, then restore with an integrity check.

Process manager

See memory/CPU use and end a process after a clear warning; critical processes are protected.

Services & startup

Start or stop non-critical services and enable/disable startup programs.

System info & monitoring

Windows version, CPU, memory and all disks, with local samples and a low-space warning.

Operations log

The PC records every action performed from the phone, without file names in the technical log.

Secure pairing

A code shown on the PC or a QR, certificate-fingerprint pinning, and unpair any phone any time.

Tray icon

Toggle the connection, start with Windows, view the log and manage paired phones from one place.

Safe self-update

The PC program verifies the SHA-256 before updating and supports rollback and offline mode.

Security & privacy

  • Direct phone↔PC connection on the local network over TLS with a pinned PC-certificate fingerprint.
  • No accounts or servers of ours; your file contents and names are never sent to anyone.
  • Each phone's token is stored on the PC as a hash and on the phone encrypted in Android Keystore.
  • Up to 10 phones, instant unpairing from the PC.

Requirements

  • Android 7.0 or later.
  • Windows 10 or 11 PC with the free Diskati companion.
  • Phone and PC on the same network.
