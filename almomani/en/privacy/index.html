Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-26

This policy applies to the “Bilal Almomani” website (https://belalmomani.github.io/GitHubPages/almomani/). Each of our apps has its own privacy policy that explains exactly what that app collects, and that policy governs that app.

1. Who we are

The person responsible for the website and apps is Bilal Almomani, Jordan. Contact: bilalalmomani90@gmail.com.

2. Website data

  • The website uses no tracking cookies, no analytics tools and no advertising.
  • There are no data-entry forms and no user accounts.
  • The website loads no fonts or scripts from third parties.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may record technical data such as IP address and browser type for operation and security under the GitHub Privacy Statement; we do not access these logs in a way that identifies you.

3. When you contact us

If you email or call us, we use your details (name, email and message content) only to reply to you. We do not sell them or share them for marketing. We keep correspondence as long as needed for follow-up and support, and delete it on request.

4. Our apps

Permissions and data differ per app. Read each app’s policy from its page in the apps list. We do not sell user data.

5. Children

The website is for a general audience and collects no data from anyone. Our children’s games explain in their own policy how children’s data is handled.

6. External links

The site may link to other sites (such as Google Play and GitHub). We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

7. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your data by contacting us. See the Data Deletion page.

8. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the “Last updated” date above when we do.
