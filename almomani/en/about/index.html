About

I’m Bilal Almomani, a software developer from Jordan building Android apps and desktop software.

Our vision

Digital tools should be simple, clear and respectful of their users’ privacy, and Arabic-speaking users deserve high-quality apps in their own language.

What we do

  • Android apps with modern interfaces.
  • Desktop software for Windows, macOS and Linux.
  • Developer tools, device-management tools and children’s games.

The logo

Our logo shows two dancers: one filled with source code and one with splashes of art, expressing that programming is a craft and creativity is what makes a tool beautiful.

Our values

  • Privacy: we request minimal permissions and explain what we collect.
  • Transparency: clear policies and data-deletion links are available.
  • Quality: we test and update continuously.
