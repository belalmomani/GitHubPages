Data & Account Deletion

Last updated: 2026-11-26

This website has no accounts and stores none of your data. If you want data linked to one of our apps deleted, follow the steps below.

1. Inside the app

If the app offers accounts or cloud sync, you’ll find an option to delete your account and data in its settings. Deleting the account in the app removes your cloud data held by us.

2. By email

Email bilalalmomani90@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion request” stating the app name and the email linked to your account. We will act within 30 days at most and confirm back to you.

3. Local data on your device

Data stored locally on your device is removed by uninstalling the app or clearing its data in device settings.

4. Third-party services

We do not control your data held by services such as GitHub or Google; contact them directly. We recommend revoking any access token you created for the app in your GitHub settings.

5. What we may retain

We may keep the minimum required by law or to protect rights, and delete it when no longer needed.
