Delete your data

Last updated: 2026-11-25

Bytezen has no accounts and no servers, so we hold no personal data to delete. Everything stored lives on your own devices and you can remove it as follows.

1. On your phone

  • Delete everything: uninstall the app, or Android Settings → Apps → Bytezen → Storage → “Clear data”.
  • Storage-monitoring samples only: in the app → Storage monitoring → clear history (also auto-deleted after 90 days).
  • Unpair: from the app, or from the PC as below.

2. On your PC

  • Unpair phones: tray icon near the clock → “Linked phones…” → unpair.
  • Stop the program: tray icon → “Stop the program completely”, and turn off “Start with Windows” if enabled.
  • Delete its data: delete the folder %LOCALAPPDATA%\Diskati (certificate, operations log, quarantine index, settings), then delete diskati.exe.
  • Note: quarantined files are removed with the folder; restore what you need first.

3. Advertising data (Start.io)

Ad data is processed by Start.io, not by us. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads; labels vary by device), and review Start.io's policy and request deletion through its website.

4. Any other request

Email bilalalmomani90@gmail.com and we will help. See also the Privacy Policy.
