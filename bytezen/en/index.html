Why Bytezen?

Private by design

No accounts, no servers. Your files move only between your phone and PC on your local network over TLS.

Safe deletion

Restorable quarantine and integrity checks before any permanent deletion, plus protection for critical processes and services.

Full control in your pocket

Disk space, processes, services, startup and an operations log from one screen.

Highlights

Get started in 4 steps

  1. Install Bytezen on your Android phone.
  2. Download diskati.exe from the download page and run it on your Windows PC; its icon appears near the clock.
  3. Pair: enter the code shown on the PC or scan the QR from the app.
  4. Manage your storage from your phone; unpair any time.

FAQ

Does Bytezen work without the PC program?

No. The app is a remote control; you need the free Diskati companion on a Windows PC. Without it the app only shows the pairing screens.

Are my files uploaded to the internet?

No. Your phone talks directly to your PC on your local network over TLS with a pinned PC-certificate fingerprint. We have no servers and no accounts.

Which systems are supported?

Android 7.0+ phones and Windows 10 or 11 PCs.

Is the app free?

Yes, free and supported by ads via Start.io. See the privacy policy for what the ad library collects.
