Frequently asked questions

Why does the app ask for "All files access"?

Because it is a file manager that browses all storage and moves, searches and organises folders, documents and archives. The permission is optional; without it the app works with the folders you grant one by one.

Does the app upload my files?

No. All analysis and indexing happen on your device and your files never pass through a server. Even transfer to a computer goes directly between your phone and your computer.

Can I recover vault files if I uninstall the app?

No. The vault is encrypted with a key stored on your device and has no backup; uninstalling or clearing the app data permanently loses its contents. Export what you want to keep first.

Is it free? Does it have ads?

It is free and contains ads, at most three times per day at calm moments.

Which Android versions are supported?

Android 8 and above. Some features need Android 10 or 11 (the vault, direct storage browsing).
