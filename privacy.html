MailSphere (ميل سفير) — com.mailsphere.app

Last updated: 5 November 2026 · آخر تحديث: 5 نوفمبر 2026

1. Who we are

MailSphere is an Android email client developed by MailSphere. You can contact us at savantmomani@gmail.com.

2. Information the app accesses

  • Account information: your email address and, when you sign in with Google or Microsoft, the access tokens needed to reach your mailbox. If you use an app password (for example with Yahoo or iCloud), that password.
  • Email data from your provider: message headers (sender, subject, date, read status), the content of messages you open, and attachments you choose to open.
  • Device and advertising data: the ad provider’s SDK may collect an advertising ID and basic device and usage information (see section 7).

3. How we use it

Only to provide the features you ask for: showing your inbox, opening, replying to, forwarding and sending messages, organizing messages on your device by sender, category and date, searching them, listing attachments, and moving messages to Trash when you tell the app to.

4. Where your data is stored

  • Sign-in credentials are stored only on your device, encrypted with Android Keystore.
  • A local index of message headers (sender, subject, date, read status, attachment names and sizes) is stored in the app’s private storage on your device. Message bodies are not stored.
  • Attachments you open are downloaded to a temporary cache on your device and removed automatically after about 24 hours.
  • We operate no servers that receive, store or process your email. The app connects directly from your phone to your mail provider over encrypted connections (TLS).

5. What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or share your email data with anyone.
  • No person at MailSphere can read your email.
  • We do not use your email content for advertising, and we do not share it with the ad provider.

6. Google user data (Limited Use)

MailSphere’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically: we use Google user data only to provide and improve user-facing features within the app; we do not transfer it to others except as necessary to provide those features, to comply with law, or as part of a merger or sale with your consent; we do not use it for advertising; and no human reads it unless you give explicit consent, it is necessary for security or legal reasons, or the data is aggregated and anonymized.

7. Third parties

  • Your mail provider (Google, Microsoft, Yahoo, Apple or others): the app communicates with it directly, and its own privacy policy applies to your mailbox.
  • Start.io (advertising): the app displays ads using the Start.io SDK, which may collect an advertising ID, device information and ad interaction data under its own privacy policy. We do not give it your email data. Ads are shown at most three times per day per device and never while you read a message.

8. Permissions

Internet access (to reach your mail provider and for ads) and access to the advertising ID. The app does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or storage.

9. Retention, deletion and your choices

  • Remove an account in the app’s Settings to delete its stored credentials and its local index from your device.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all data it stored on your device.
  • You can revoke MailSphere’s access at any time: for Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions; for Microsoft at account.live.com/consent/Manage.
  • Moving messages to Trash happens only when you confirm it, and can usually be undone from your provider’s Trash folder.

10. Security

We use encrypted connections, Android Keystore encryption for credentials, and the Android app sandbox. No method is perfectly secure; keep your phone locked and updated.

11. Children

MailSphere is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

12. Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version. Questions: savantmomani@gmail.com.
