Delete the app and your data

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Terminal AR needs no account and we do not store your data on our servers, so there is no account to delete. Everything the app saves is on your device, and you remove it as follows:

Android

  1. Clear data only: Settings → Apps → Terminal AR → Storage → Clear data.
  2. Delete the app and all its data: long-press the app icon → Uninstall.

Windows

  1. Settings → Apps → Terminal AR → Uninstall.
  2. To remove leftover data, delete the com.terminalar.app folder inside %APPDATA% and %LOCALAPPDATA% if present.

macOS and Linux

  1. Delete the app from Applications (or remove the package with your package manager).
  2. Delete the app-data folder named com.terminalar.app from your user app-data location (for example ~/Library/Application Support on macOS and ~/.local/share on Linux).

Folder names differ slightly between systems and versions; look for the folder named com.terminalar.app.

Ad data (Android)

Ads are served by the Start.io SDK, and its data is held by Start.io, not by us. To control it:

  • Reset your advertising ID: Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID, or delete it.
  • Opt out of ads personalization on the same screen.
  • To request deletion of data held by Start.io, see its privacy practices.
