What the app offers

Everything listed here exists in the app today. What is not done yet is stated plainly under “Honest limits”.

Full Arabic interface

Menus, messages and errors in Arabic, plus a text view that shows terminal output with correctly connected Arabic letters.

Workflow tools

Define a tool with a manifest, chain tools into workflows, run them in one tap and watch the results.

Per-permission plugins

.tarpkg packages get no permission automatically. You approve each permission separately, and it is recorded in an audit log.

Phone ↔ computer pairing

Unlock the desktop app by scanning a QR code with your phone. The connection is direct and encrypted between your two devices, over your local network or a VPN such as Tailscale.

Device discovery

The phone finds your computer on the local network (mDNS) without typing addresses; manual entry always works too.

Studio editor

Create and edit tools inside the app, with manifest validation before saving.

How the phone lock works

  1. Turn the lock on in the desktop app (optional, off by default).
  2. A QR screen appears with a single-use code valid for two minutes.
  3. Scan it with the phone app and the desktop opens.
  4. It stays open while the phone stays connected. If the connection drops or the app closes, the desktop locks again and needs a new pairing.

Security and privacy

  • No account or sign-in, and we run no server that receives your data.
  • Device-to-device connections are end-to-end encrypted (Noise protocol) and never pass through our servers.
  • Pairing refuses public Internet addresses; only local networks and VPNs are accepted.
  • Passwords are never stored in plaintext.
  • A plugin being listed in the store grants it no permission; approval always comes from you.

Honest limits

  • Arabic rendering inside the terminal itself may not be perfect in every case, because the terminal library has no official Arabic shaping support; that is why we added the text view.
  • Connecting over the public Internet without a VPN is not supported yet (no relay server).
  • Encryption and approvals reduce risk but do not guarantee absolute security.

FAQ

Is the app free?

Yes. The Android version shows ads, at most 3 per 24 hours, to support development.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything runs on your device with no sign-up.

Do I need both the desktop and phone apps?

Each works on its own. If you turn on the phone lock for your computer, you need both.

How do I delete the app and my data?

See Delete the app and your data.

What data do the ads collect?

See the Privacy Policy: advertising ID, IP address and device information through the Start.io SDK, in the Android version only.
