Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026 · Developer: Bilal Almomani · bilalalmomani90@gmail.com

1. What the app is

Terminal AR (package com.terminalar.app) is a terminal app with an Arabic interface for running commands, tools and workflows on your device. It needs no account, and we operate no server that receives your data.

2. Data that stays on your device

The app stores locally only: its settings, tool definitions you install or write, a permission audit log, a device identity key, and fingerprints of paired devices. None of this is sent to us. What you type in the terminal and its output stay on your device; we do not collect them.

3. Connections between your devices

When you pair your phone with the desktop app, the connection is direct between your two devices over your local network or a VPN you use, end-to-end encrypted, and does not pass through our servers. The app refuses public Internet addresses for this connection. While looking for nearby devices, the app sends and receives local-network discovery messages (mDNS) containing only the device name and port.

4. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to reach your other device and to show ads.
  • Camera: only to scan a QR code when unlocking your computer. Images are not saved or sent.
  • Wi‑Fi state and multicast: to find your devices on the local network.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID): used by the advertising library described below.

The app does not request location, contacts, SMS or microphone permissions.

5. Advertising (Android version)

The Android version shows ads through the Start.io (StartApp) SDK, at most 3 ads per 24 hours. The SDK may collect and share with Start.io and its advertising partners: the Android advertising ID, the IP address (and approximate location derived from it), device and app information needed to serve and measure ads, and crash diagnostics. We do not access this data. See Start.io's privacy practices.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in Android settings (Google → Ads). Desktop versions contain no ads.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

7. Security

Device-to-device connections are encrypted (Noise protocol) and passwords are never stored in plaintext. No system guarantees absolute security.

8. Deleting your data

Because we do not store your data on our servers, uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it. Steps are on the Delete the app and your data page.
