About Movies Selection

An app built by an independent developer to solve a problem everyone who loves movies knows: losing time choosing.

Movies Selection is developed by Bilal almomani. The idea began with a simple observation: choosing sometimes takes longer than watching. So the app was designed as an assistant that asks, understands and then suggests, not just a library.

Our principles

  • Privacy first: no account, no ads, and your data stays on your device.
  • Honest data: sourced from TMDB and Wikidata, which we credit, and estimates are called estimates.
  • Simplicity: a few questions and clear results.
  • For everyone: Arabic and English, phone and TV.
