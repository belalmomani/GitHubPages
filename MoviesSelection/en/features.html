Movies Selection features

Every one of these ways leads to the same goal: a title that fits you tonight.

Choosing what to watch tonight

Smart filter

Instead of typing a title, you answer a chain of simple questions: movie or series? Arabic or foreign? Which genre? Which era? What minimum rating? The app picks the next question that narrows the results the most. You can exclude what you do not want, undo any choice, save a filter to reuse it, and see how many titles TMDB has for your answers as you go.

Choose for me

For the moment when you do not know what you want. Three questions about how you feel, then two more if needed, and you land on the Three Doors experience. The goal is to end the indecision quickly.

By my mood

Pick your mood or the atmosphere you want and the app turns it into search conditions, then shows matching titles.

My taste

It shows one title at a time: you answer yes or no and say why. Over time a personal taste profile (genres you like and avoid) builds up and stays on your device.

Mystery box

Pure surprise: three titles without names, showing only their genres and content warnings, so you discover something new without being swayed by its name or fame.

Three doors

Three directions to choose from: closest to your taste, a calculated surprise, and the safe pick. An interactive, fun choice instead of a long list.

Smart roulette

A draw among eligible titles that is not purely random: the best fits get a bigger chance, with a little room for surprise. A title that breaks a condition you set is never in the draw.

Tune my preferences

Sliders to shape the experience: calm or tense, realistic or fantastical, light or deep. You can also exclude things like horror, betrayal, sad endings, blood, school settings or long shows. These estimates are inferred from a title's genres, so they are approximate rather than official facts.

Party night

For two people with different tastes who want to watch together: the app intersects both people's preferences and suggests something that suits them both.

Discovery and recommendations

Hidden gems

Good titles that are not necessarily the most famous: rated 7 or higher with roughly 1,500 to 20,000 votes, ranked by a vote-weighted rating.

Cinematic worlds web

Start from one title and hop between related ones: a shared actor, director or creator, keywords, story universe, genre, or TMDB recommendations. Titles appear as a network, and the one you pick becomes its new centre.

Cinematic time gate

An interactive experience for exploring titles across 13 time and theme stations: the future, ancient Rome, the Middle Ages, dinosaurs, ancient Egypt, the Vikings, the American West, pirates, World War II, the Cold War, cyberpunk, post-apocalypse and classic cinema. It has a cinematic intro, interactive scenes, a time radar, speed control and a time hole that shows titles from different stations. The scenes are drawn inside the app: a visual simulation, not a full 3D game engine.

What you watched

Mark what you have watched and the app suggests more of the same: if most of it is action you get action suggestions, if comedy then comedy. Your watched marks stay on your device only.

Daily lists

On the home screen: 10 movies and 10 series every day rated 8 or higher, and 10 actors every day rated 7.5 or higher. An actor's rating here is the average TMDB rating of their best-known titles, because TMDB has no rating for the person themselves.

Details and tools

Title page

The story, rating, runtime, seasons and episodes, cast and crew, where to watch (from JustWatch via TMDB) and similar titles. Plus favourites, watch later, a watched mark, sharing and side-by-side comparison of two titles.

Search and my lists

Search for a movie, series or person. Keep your titles in local lists on your device: favourites, watch later and watched.

Two languages, two kinds of device

Arabic and English with full right-to-left support, light and dark themes. Works on Android phones and on Android TV / Google TV with a remote.
