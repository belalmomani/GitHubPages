Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to what people ask most about the app.

Is the app free?

Yes, completely free. It shows no ads and asks for no subscription.

Do I need an account or to sign in?

No. There is no account and no login, and we have no server that receives your data.

Where are my favourites and lists stored?

On your device only. You can erase them with "Erase my local data" in Settings, by clearing the app's storage, or by uninstalling the app.

Where does the movie and series data come from?

From TMDB (The Movie Database), plus Wikidata to help discover Arabic productions. Where-to-watch data comes from JustWatch via TMDB.

Is the app endorsed by TMDB?

No. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

Does it work on a TV?

Yes, on Android TV and Google TV with a remote. By default it shows the same design as the phone, and Settings has an optional simplified TV edition.

Which Android version do I need?

Android 8.0 (API level 26) or newer.

Why does a certain title not show up in my results?

The filter relies on what TMDB knows about each title. A title with incomplete data is not dropped automatically but listed as "possible" where it can be. If you see a mistake, write to us.

How do I update the app?

Download the newest file from the download page and install it over the old one; your favourites and lists are kept.

How do I delete the app and my data?

Follow the steps on the "Delete the app and your data" page. There is no data of yours on our servers to delete.
