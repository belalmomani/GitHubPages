Privacy policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

App: Movies Selection (com.tvseries.Movies)
Developer: Bilal almomani — Contact: bilalalmomani90@gmail.com

1. Summary

  • No account, no login, and no server of ours that receives your data.
  • Your favourites, lists, taste profile and settings stay on your device only.
  • The app is free and shows no ads, contains no advertising library and does not use the advertising ID.
  • Intended for people aged 18 and over.

2. Stored on your device (never sent to us)

Favourites, watch-later list, watched marks and the genres of titles you marked (to suggest similar ones), saved filters, last filter, preferences and learned taste (genres you accepted/rejected), language/theme/TV-edition settings, search history (off by default) and an optional TMDB key you typed yourself. Erase it all with "Erase my local data" in Settings, by clearing app storage, or by uninstalling. See Delete the app and your data.

3. External services the app contacts

4. Ads

The app shows no ads of any kind, shares your data with no advertising network and does not collect your device's advertising ID.

5. Permissions

  • INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: fetch data.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: optional weekly reminder, off by default, created locally.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: re-schedule that reminder after a reboot, only if enabled.

6. Children

Not directed to children; intended for ages 18+. Adult content is hidden by default. If you learn that a child used the app, write to us.

7. Retention, deletion and your rights

We keep no data on a server, so we hold nothing to delete or export; everything about you is local and fully under your control (erase data in Settings or uninstall). Data collected by the third parties above is governed by their policies, and you can exercise your rights (access/deletion/objection) with them directly.

8. Security

Connections to external services use HTTPS. We store no passwords, payment data or location.

9. Attribution

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. Where-to-watch data is provided by JustWatch via TMDB. Wikidata is available under CC0. Platform/title names and artwork belong to their owners; their appearance does not imply sponsorship.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version. Questions: bilalalmomani90@gmail.com.
