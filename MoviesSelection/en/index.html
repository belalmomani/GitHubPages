Pick your movie or series, one question at a time

Movies Selection is an Android app that gets you to the right title for your mood and taste, instead of drowning in thousands of movies and series. Free, no ads, no account, in Arabic and English, for phones and TVs.

The problem we solve

You open a streaming app, see thousands of titles, scroll for half an hour and close it without watching anything. Movies Selection does not want to be another library or a "most watched" list. It is a personal assistant for choosing: it asks, understands what you want, then suggests titles that fit.

Different ways to reach the right title

Smart filter

Instead of typing a title, you answer a chain of simple questions: movie or series? Arabic or foreign? Which genre? Which era? What minimum rating? The app picks the next question that narrows the results the most. You can exclude what you do not want, undo any choice, save a filter to reuse it, and see how many titles TMDB has for your answers as you go.

Choose for me

For the moment when you do not know what you want. Three questions about how you feel, then two more if needed, and you land on the Three Doors experience. The goal is to end the indecision quickly.

By my mood

Pick your mood or the atmosphere you want and the app turns it into search conditions, then shows matching titles.

My taste

It shows one title at a time: you answer yes or no and say why. Over time a personal taste profile (genres you like and avoid) builds up and stays on your device.

Mystery box

Pure surprise: three titles without names, showing only their genres and content warnings, so you discover something new without being swayed by its name or fame.

Party night

For two people with different tastes who want to watch together: the app intersects both people's preferences and suggests something that suits them both.
