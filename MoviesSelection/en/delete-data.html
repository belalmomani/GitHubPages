Delete the app and your data

The app needs no account and we keep no data of yours on a server, so everything about you is on your device and fully under your control.

Option 1: erase your data and keep the app

  1. Open the app → Settings.
  2. Tap Erase my local data.

This removes favourites, the watch-later list, watched marks, saved filters, the taste profile and preferences.

Option 2: clear the app's data from Android

  1. Android Settings → Apps → Movies Selection.
  2. Storage → Clear data.

Option 3: uninstall the app completely

  1. Android Settings → Apps → Movies Selection → Uninstall.
  2. Or long-press the app icon and choose Uninstall.
  3. On a TV: Apps → Movies Selection → Uninstall.

Uninstalling removes all of the app's local data from the device.

What about data held by external services?

The app contacts TMDB, Wikidata and YouTube to fetch data and play trailers; those parties manage what they collect under their own policies, and we do not hold it. See the privacy policy.
