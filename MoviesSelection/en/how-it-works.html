How Movies Selection works

A simple idea: a few questions turn a vague wish into clear conditions, then into real titles from a large database.

  1. 1. You answerPick the way that suits you: the smart filter with a chain of questions, choose-for-me with feeling questions, by my mood, or the mystery box. You do not need to know a title.
  2. 2. We turn answers into conditionsWithin one question, any answer you pick is enough (action or comedy, say); across different questions all of them must hold (a movie, Arabic, highly rated). You can exclude genres or traits and undo any step.
  3. 3. We ask TMDB for titlesThe app sends the conditions TMDB can filter itself (genre, country, language, year, rating, runtime, keywords, and cast and director for movies), so you get real titles and a count of matches. The remaining conditions are checked on your device.
  4. 4. Missing data does not exclude a titleIf a title's data is incomplete we do not drop it automatically; it goes into the possible results so a good title is not lost.
  5. 5. Your taste stays with youWhat you mark as favourite, watched or taste is stored on your device only, and personal suggestions are built from it.

Note: estimates such as "calm or tense" or "realistic or fantastical" are inferred from a title's genres, so they are approximate rather than official data.

Data sources

Movies, series, images and cast come from TMDB, where-to-watch data from JustWatch via TMDB, and some Arabic titles are found with the help of Wikidata. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
